# 🍫 Chocolate Shipments – Power BI Business Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an end-to-end **Power BI business analytics solution** built on a chocolate shipments dataset.
It focuses on **revenue concentration, profitability risks, salesperson performance, shipment trends, and YoY growth analysis** to support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard is designed for **senior management and business stakeholders** to quickly identify growth drivers, risks, and optimization opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Identify **revenue concentration and dependency risks**
- Detect **loss-making products and regions**
- Evaluate **salesperson performance & efficiency**
- Analyze **shipment growth, seasonality, and volatility**
- Compare **YoY revenue performance and variance**

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI**
- **Microsoft Excel**
- **DAX (Time Intelligence, Rolling Metrics, YoY, MoM)**
- **Data Modeling & Business KPIs**
